# Ernesto Sábato

## One-line frame
"Poor man's Dostoevsky" — psychological obsession that already knows despair is the answer. *El túnel* is *Werther* compressed and stripped of Romantic beauty. The narrowing is the method.

## Works read
- *El túnel*
- *Sobre héroes y tumbas* (partial / long-ago)

## Genealogy
- [[Dostoevsky, Fyodor]] — "poor man's Dostoevsky" is the frame Sábato lives in and never escapes.

## Parallels
- [[Goethe, Johann Wolfgang]] — draining reads at the same age; Werther and El Túnel both obsessive spirals.
- Sartre — consciousness-as-trap.
- Ingenieros — Argentine pessimism.

## In tension with
- Camus — absurd without revolt. Sábato stays inside the trap.

## Notes
Satellite. Read in adolescence, drained; may not reward a return.
