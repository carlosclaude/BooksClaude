# Aldous Huxley

## One-line frame
Pleasure as control. *Brave New World* as dystopia of ambient normalcy — the system does not require violence because it requires no resistance. Late mystical turn suggests the critique's other side.

## Works read
- *Brave New World*
- *The Doors of Perception* (partial)

## Parallels

- Hesse — mystical turn.
- Murakami — ambient dystopia.
