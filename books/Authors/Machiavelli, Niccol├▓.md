# Niccolò Machiavelli

## One-line frame
Power as it IS, not as it should be. *The Prince* as realpolitik foundation; *Discourses on Livy* as the republican counterweight most readers ignore. Napoleon annotated *The Prince* as practitioner engaging with theory.

## Parallels
- Da Empoli — modern realpolitik in fictional form, direct lineage.
