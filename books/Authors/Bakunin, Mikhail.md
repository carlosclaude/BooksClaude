# Mikhail Bakunin

## One-line frame
Collectivist anarchism. The split with Marx at the First International (1872) is the foundational left schism: centralized revolution vs. decentralized liberation.

## In tension with
- [[Marx, Karl]] — the foundational break.

- [[Pynchon, Thomas]] — Lot 49's Tristero as decentralized shadow network; Bakunin's decentralized liberation rendered as paranoid fiction.
