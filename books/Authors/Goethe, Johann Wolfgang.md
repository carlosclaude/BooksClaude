# Johann Wolfgang von Goethe

## One-line frame
*Faust* and *Werther* as foundational templates. The sensitive young man as self-annihilation engine (Werther). The intellectual who has consumed everything and found it insufficient (Faust). Wrote *Werther* at 24 and survived the impulse by writing it.

## Works read
- *Faust* (parts I and II)
- *The Sorrows of Young Werther*

## Genealogy
- [[Mann, Thomas]] — Mann is Goethe's most self-conscious heir. *Doctor Faustus* is literally Faust retold for German culture's descent into Nazism.
- [[Dazai, Osamu]] — *Werther* → *No Longer Human*. Two centuries, same architecture.
- Hesse — German lineage; *Steppenwolf* references Faust explicitly.

## Parallels
- [[Dostoevsky, Fyodor]] — Faustian architecture. Raskolnikov's murder as Faustian experiment.
- [[Awad, Mona]] — Faustian bargain as deep structure. *Rouge* trades something essential for something desired; Goethe formalized the template.
- [[Nietzsche, Friedrich]] — lived affirmation. Nietzsche admired Goethe as one of the few humans who actually lived the affirmation he philosophized about.
- Sábato — draining reads; *Werther* and *El túnel* as obsessive spirals that drain the reader.

## In tension with
- Camus — opposite responses to the absurd. Faust refuses through total knowledge. Sisyphus accepts through labor.
