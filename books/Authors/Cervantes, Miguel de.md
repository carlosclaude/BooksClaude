# Miguel de Cervantes

## One-line frame
*Don Quixote*: the origin. The novel as self-aware form from the moment of its birth. The quest template and its ironic destabilization arrive together in Western literature — already self-critical.

## Works read
- *Don Quixote*

## Genealogy
- [[Tolkien, J.R.R.]] — quest template, inherited and un-ironized.
- [[Aira, César]] — quest absurdity. Aira licensed by Cervantes's establishment that narrative logic can be a thought experiment's deliverable.

## Parallels
- [[Borges, Jorge Luis]] — self-aware fiction. Borges returned to Cervantes repeatedly as ancestor of everything Borgesian.
- [[Pynchon, Thomas]] — quest deconstructed. The Crying of Lot 49 is Quixote's quest in a post-Californian key.
- [[Kundera, Milan]] — novel as form. Kundera's *Art of the Novel* is essentially Cervantes-centered.
- Steinbeck — Arthurian inheritance (via *Acts of King Arthur*).
