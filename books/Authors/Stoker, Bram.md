# Bram Stoker

## One-line frame
*Dracula* as Gothic origin: blood as medium of exchange, mirrors as unreliable perception, sun as destroyer. Harker's journal fragmentation = epistemological collapse. The horror is seduction, not force — the victim wants the consumption.

## Works read
- *Dracula*

## Genealogy
- [[Awad, Mona]] — blood → beauty exchange. *Rouge* inherits *Dracula*'s full architecture: blood/beauty exchange, mirrors, sun as enemy, victim wanting the consumption. Updated from aristocratic vampire to beauty industry.
- King — domestic Gothic. Gothic horror rooted in houses, families, intimate spaces. Dracula's castle → Overlook Hotel.
- Female Gothic — Gothic origin. The lineage Stoker enables for female writers reclaiming body-horror.

## In tension with
- [[Lovecraft, H.P.]] — paired horror origins. Intimate/bodily/seductive (Stoker) vs. cosmic/impersonal/incomprehensible (Lovecraft). Two foundational branches of genre.
