# Arturo Jauretche

## One-line frame
*Medio pelo*: the imitation of European culture IS the mediocrity. Kundera's kitsch applied to class performance — ideology operating through taste and habits, not belief.

## Parallels
- [[Kundera, Milan]] — kitsch as class performance, Central European formulation.
- [[Žižek, Slavoj]] — ideology of taste.
- [[Han, Byung-Chul]] — performance as internalized obligation.
- [[Martí, José]] — Latin American anti-colonial intellectual lineage.
- [[Galeano, Eduardo]] — applied anti-imperialism; shared instinct.
