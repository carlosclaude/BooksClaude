# Michel Foucault

## One-line frame
Episteme: the unthought conditions of thought at a given historical moment. Panopticon internalized — the subject disciplines itself. Power operates through procedure, not violence.

## Genealogical
- [[Han, Byung-Chul]] — power internalized becomes self-exploitation. Han extends Foucault into late-capitalist subjectivity.
- [[Fisher, Mark]] — capitalist realism as episteme applied to economics. Fisher's "we can't imagine alternatives" is Foucault's episteme-work.

## Parallels
- [[Kafka, Franz]] — *The Castle* and *The Trial* are the literary form of Foucault's analysis.
- [[Pynchon, Thomas]] — panopticon as paranoia made theoretical.
- Orwell — *1984*'s telescreen is Foucault's panopticon as fiction.

## In tension with
- [[Žižek, Slavoj]] — genuine methodological disagreement about how power and ideology operate.
