# George Orwell

## One-line frame
Pain as control. *Animal Farm* as critique of what Leninism becomes. *Homage to Catalonia* as journalism becoming philosophy — the writer as actor in the Spanish Civil War.

## Works read
- *1984*
- *Animal Farm*
- *Homage to Catalonia*

## In tension with
- Huxley — pain vs. pleasure as mechanism. *1984* and *Brave New World* as the two branches of dystopia.

## Parallels
- [[Kundera, Milan]] — totalitarianism examined from outside what Kundera examines from inside.
- Camus — political engagement.
- Hemingway — Spanish Civil War experience.
- [[Foucault, Michel]] — surveillance theory; telescreen as panopticon made literal.
- [[Martí, José]] — writer as actor.
- [[Galeano, Eduardo]] — journalism as philosophy, different continents.

## Notes
Satellite. The essays (*Politics and the English Language*, etc.) are probably the center of the corpus; the novels are famous but less distinctive.
