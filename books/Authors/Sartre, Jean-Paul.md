# Jean-Paul Sartre

## One-line frame
Strips theology, keeps phenomenological core. *Nausea*: consciousness when meaning collapses. Confronts the absurd through engagement, commitment, political action — the opposite of Camus's exit through revolt and art.

## Works read
- *La Nausée* / *Nausea*
- *No Exit*

## In tension with
- Camus — the famous split over political violence. Same diagnosis, opposite prescriptions on engagement.

## Parallels
- [[Wallace, David Foster]] — consciousness-as-trap. Sartre's *Nausea* and DFW's footnotes are different technologies for the same claustrophobia.
- [[Sábato, Ernesto]] — consciousness-as-trap continues.
- Hemingway — hidden existentialism in American prose.
