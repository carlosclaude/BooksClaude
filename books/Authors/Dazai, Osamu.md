# Osamu Dazai

## One-line frame
Japanese Dostoevsky — existentialism internalized as self-destruction. The outlier who performs normalcy so desperately that the performance consumes him.

## Works read
- *No Longer Human*
- *The Setting Sun*

## Genealogy
- [[Dostoevsky, Fyodor]] — *No Longer Human* as *Notes from Underground* transposed into post-war Japanese shame culture.
- [[Goethe, Johann Wolfgang]] — *Werther* → *No Longer Human*. Two centuries apart, same architecture — young man's sensitivity becomes mechanism of destruction.

## Parallels
- Salinger — sensitive self-annihilation.

## In tension with
- [[Murata, Sayaka]] — outlier types. Yozo consumed by the performance. Keiko efficient, unsuffering. New category.

## Notes
Satellite. The Japanese Dostoevsky thread is the center; the rest of the corpus (*The Setting Sun* etc.) lesser.
