# Isaiah Berlin

## One-line frame
Liberal pluralism as answer to Enlightenment monism. *Two Concepts of Liberty*: negative vs. positive freedom. Specifically designed to counter the Marx-Hegel-Lenin tradition — the anti-totalitarian liberal thinker who did not surrender the complexity of the position.

## In tension with
- [[Marx, Karl]] — the opposition is structural, not incidental.
- [[Machiavelli, Niccolò]] — Berlin's "The Originality of Machiavelli" reads realpolitik as the fracture point in Western ethical tradition.

## Parallels
- *The Proper Study of Mankind* as anthology — the best single entry to Berlin's range.
