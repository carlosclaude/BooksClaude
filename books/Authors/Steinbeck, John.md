# John Steinbeck

## One-line frame
American landscape with Faulkner's weight and Hemingway's prose clarity. *East of Eden* as *The Brothers Karamazov* transplanted to the Salinas Valley. Also: *The Acts of King Arthur* as modernized Malory — the Arthurian project that connects Steinbeck to the quest lineage.

## Works read
- *East of Eden*
- *The Grapes of Wrath*
- (partial — *Of Mice and Men*, probably)

## Parallels
- [[Faulkner, William]] — American landscape and social weight.
- Hemingway — prose kinship.
- [[Dostoevsky, Fyodor]] — moral architecture. *East of Eden* is *Karamazov* in California; Cain and Abel in Salinas.
- Cervantes / Arthurian — *The Acts of King Arthur* as the modernization of Malory.
