# Greg Egan

## One-line frame
Philosopher of consciousness using fiction. Thought experiments as novels — hard physics, harder metaphysics. Better ideas than execution; the novels are conceptual-demos, not characters-dramas.

## Works read
- *Permutation City*
- (additional Egan across short fiction)

## Parallels
- [[Borges, Jorge Luis]] — thought experiments at different scale.
- Asimov — ideas over characters.
- [[Pynchon, Thomas]] — hidden systems (weak parallel).

## In tension with
- Heidegger — death made optional. Egan's uploaded-consciousness premise is the technological refutation of Heidegger's being-toward-death — whether it actually refutes it or just defers it is the live question.
- [[Philip K. Dick]] — opposite approaches. Dick asks "what if reality is fake?" Egan asks "if we can simulate consciousness, does it matter which substrate it runs on?" Same problem, opposite technological optimism.
- [[Han, Byung-Chul]] — eternal optimization as dystopia.
