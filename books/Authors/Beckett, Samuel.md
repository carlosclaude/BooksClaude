# Samuel Beckett

## One-line frame
The absurd stripped to minimum. Godot that never arrives. Hemingway's iceberg with the surface removed entirely — not meaning hidden beneath, but meaning abolished while the waiting continues.

## Works read

## Parallels
- Camus — absurd as theater. Sisyphus and Godot as the same proposition in different form.
- [[Kafka, Franz]] — waiting without resolution. The Castle and Godot as the same condition.
- Hemingway — radical economy.
- [[Murata, Sayaka]] — repetition as meaning. Is repetition meaningless or the only meaning available?
- [[Krasznahorkai, László]] — stripped endurance.
