# Ayn Rand

## One-line frame
Objectivism: rational self-interest as moral absolute. Nietzsche flattened into economics — Übermensch stripped of ambiguity and replaced with rational self-interest. Worth reading; not worth agreeing with.

## Works read
- *The Fountainhead*
- *Atlas Shrugged*

## In tension with
- [[Nietzsche, Friedrich]] — flattened inheritance. Rand takes Nietzsche's ambiguity out and sells the residue.
- [[Kundera, Milan]] — refusal of the draft condition. Rand does Nietzsche as finished product; Kundera does Nietzsche as permanent draft. The contrast is exact.
- [[Wallace, David Foster]] — Rand cannot hold seriousness and comedy simultaneously (the Kafka problem). Her framework requires seriousness to be serious.

## Notes
Satellite. Performs kitsch in Kundera's sense: refuses the draft condition; presents finished ideological product. Included in the network as the negative pole of the Nietzsche lineage.
