# Søren Kierkegaard

## One-line frame
Indirect communication; the leap of faith; the absurd encountered religiously rather than existentially. Origin node for the "truth can only be transmitted obliquely" tradition that runs through Kundera and Pynchon.

## Parallels
- [[Kundera, Milan]] — irony as philosophical method.
- [[Pynchon, Thomas]] — indirect communication as structural principle of Lot 49.

## In tension with
- [[Nietzsche, Friedrich]] — opposite rebellions against systematic philosophy. Faith vs. will to power.

## Parallels
- [[Camus, Albert]] — the absurd. Camus secularizes what Kierkegaard keeps religious.

## Notes
Satellite. The *Either/Or* and *Fear and Trembling* tradition is acknowledged but not yet read directly enough to deepen.
