# Eduardo Galeano

## One-line frame
*Open Veins of Latin America*: journalism becoming philosophy; history from the margins. Marxism applied to the specific conditions of Latin American extraction.

## Genealogy
- [[Marx, Karl]] — applied to extraction. *Open Veins* is Marxist analysis applied to Latin American resource extraction and colonial exploitation.

## Parallels
- Orwell — journalism becoming philosophy, same method across continents.
- [[Martí, José]] — Latin American liberation intellectual tradition.
- Jauretche — anti-colonial kin; Argentine and Uruguayan voices of the tradition.

## In tension with
- Zweig — opposite vantage points. Both write history as literary narrative; Zweig from European center, Galeano from Latin American periphery.
