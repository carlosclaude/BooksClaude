# Hermann Hesse

## One-line frame
Bridge between Russian existential depth and contemplative Eastern philosophy. Dostoevsky who meditates instead of screams. Read completely in adolescence — ideal timing for Hesse, whose insights land hardest at 15–20.

## Works read
- *Steppenwolf*
- *Siddhartha*
- *Demian*
- *The Glass Bead Game*
- *Narcissus and Goldmund*
- (effectively the complete adolescent corpus)

## Parallels
- [[Nietzsche, Friedrich]] — *Steppenwolf* is Nietzschean crisis rendered as novel.
- [[Goethe, Johann Wolfgang]] — German inheritance; *Steppenwolf* references Faust explicitly.
- [[Mann, Thomas]] — German contemporaries; different temperatures on the same civilizational crisis.
- [[Kundera, Milan]] — both write novels where existence itself is the subject.
- Huxley — mystical turn, Eastern contemplation.
- [[Tolkien, J.R.R.]] — quest inwardness.
