# Toni Morrison

## One-line frame
*Beloved*: 124 Bluestone Road as selective-space architecture. Supernatural escalation that surfaces what was always there — not invasion, revelation. Family love as the structural exit from a closed system of damage.

- [[Awad, Mona]] — selective-space architecture. 124 Bluestone ↔ the spa in *Rouge*. Spaces with agency that refuse some entrants.
