# Leo Tolstoy

## One-line frame
Philosophy and history in perfect balance — the smoke at Austerlitz says history is chaos, not system. Christian anarchist in life; author-God in fiction. The author who never sacrifices Natasha for theory.

## Works read
- *War and Peace*
- *Anna Karenina*
- *The Death of Ivan Ilyich*
- *Hadji Murat* (verify)

## In tension with
- [[Dostoevsky, Fyodor]] — Russian poles. Moral vision clarified through narrative (Tolstoy) vs. polyphony without author-god (Dostoevsky). Same tradition, opposite form.
- Hemingway — primary rival. Tolstoy's expansive moral universe vs. Hemingway's iceberg.
- Zweig — the smoke vs. the great moment.

## Parallels
- [[Sebald, W.G.]] — the Austerlitz smoke as direct lineage.
- [[Mann, Thomas]] — civilization in individuals. *Magic Mountain* as the sanatorium what *War and Peace* does on the battlefield.
- Asimov — history as math. Psychohistory as Tolstoy's history translated into science-fiction formula.
- [[Schopenhauer, Arthur]] — late Tolstoy absorbed Schopenhauer's pessimism.
