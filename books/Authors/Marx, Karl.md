# Karl Marx

## One-line frame
Hegel inverted into dialectical materialism. Structural conditions produce predictable outcomes without conspiracy — the 19th-century ancestor of the [[Sedimentation model]].

## Parallels
- [[Fisher, Mark]] — capitalist realism as Marxist analysis updated for the era of imaginative closure.
- [[Galeano, Eduardo]] — Marxism applied to Latin American resource extraction.

## In tension with
- [[Bakunin, Mikhail]] — First International split (1872). Centralized revolution vs. decentralized liberation — foundational left schism.
- [[Berlin, Isaiah]] — liberal pluralism specifically built to counter the Marx-Hegel-Lenin tradition.

## Genealogical
- [[Lenin, Vladimir]] — Marx's theory → praxis.

Satellite. The *Capital* Vol. 1 and *Manifesto* rubric is partial; the deeper Marx (*Grundrisse*, the later manuscripts) not yet read. The sedimentation-model lineage is the live interest.
