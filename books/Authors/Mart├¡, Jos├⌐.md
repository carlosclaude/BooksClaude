# José Martí

## One-line frame
Poet-revolutionary. Cuba libre. The writer as political actor — pen and action as the same commitment.

## Parallels
- Orwell — writer as actor (Homage to Catalonia; Martí's revolution).
- Camus — engaged rebel. Both combine artistic and political commitment without subordinating one to the other.
- [[Bakunin, Mikhail]] — liberation movements, different targets.
- [[Galeano, Eduardo]] — Latin American anti-colonial intellectual lineage.
- Jauretche — Latin American voice.

## In tension with
- [[Lenin, Vladimir]] — theory into praxis through opposite methods.
