# José Ingenieros

## One-line frame
*El hombre mediocre*: Argentine Nietzscheanism at the turn of the 20th century. Closer to Rand's flattening than Nietzsche's ambiguity, but more honest about aspiration and its cost.

## Genealogical
- [[Nietzsche, Friedrich]] — Argentine reception of Nietzsche's herd/Übermensch distinction, applied to national context.

## In tension with
- Jauretche — Argentine opposites. Ingenieros: rise above the mediocre (Nietzschean elitism). Jauretche: the imitation of European culture IS the mediocrity. Same Argentine condition, opposite diagnoses.

## Parallels
- [[Borges, Jorge Luis]] — Argentine cosmopolitan intellectual tradition.
- [[Sábato, Ernesto]] — Argentine pessimism, different register.
