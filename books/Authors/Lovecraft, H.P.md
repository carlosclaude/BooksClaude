# H.P. Lovecraft

## One-line frame
Cosmic horror: the universe is indifferent, incomprehensible, and hostile. Opposite of Stoker's intimate bodily horror — the terror isn't that something wants you, it's that nothing does. Horror from scale, not proximity.
