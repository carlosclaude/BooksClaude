# Stephen King

## One-line frame
Domestic horror maximalist. Overlook = Casa Tomada with ghosts. Archetypal characters as vessels for moral propositions; *The Long Walk* as the most Hemingway-adjacent.

## Works read
- *The Shining*
- *The Long Walk* (as Bachman)
- *It* (partial / verify)

## Parallels

- Hemingway — *Long Walk* kinship. People walking forward through a landscape trying to kill them.
- [[McCarthy, Cormac]] — archetypal vessels.
- [[Awad, Mona]] — maximalist horror.
