# J.D. Salinger

## One-line frame
Withdrawal as gesture. *Nine Stories*: the epiphany withheld. "For Esmé" as the chance meeting that saves a life months later — Kundera's contingency architecture avant la lettre.

## Works read
- *The Catcher in the Rye*
- *Nine Stories*

## Parallels
- Hemingway — prose discipline.
- [[Dazai, Osamu]] — sensitive self-annihilation.
- [[Wallace, David Foster]] — sincerity problem; DFW as Salinger's successor in writing self-aware American consciousness.
- [[Kundera, Milan]] — contingency saves. "For Esmé": chance meeting before D-Day becomes load-bearing months later. Kundera's accidental-encounter architecture.
- [[Cusk, Rachel]] — fragment as person. "De Daumier-Smith" constructs an entire person from a fragment. Cusk's *Outline* is the mature version.
- [[Murata, Sayaka]] — withdrawal as logistics; Seymour Glass as the efficient outlier avant la lettre.
