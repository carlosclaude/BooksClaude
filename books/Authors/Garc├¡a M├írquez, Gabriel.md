# Gabriel García Márquez

## One-line frame
Myth saturating reality. *One Hundred Years of Solitude* as generational epic; magical realism as cultural default, not ironic gesture. The Boom's most famous output.

## Works read
- *Cien años de soledad*
- *El amor en los tiempos del cólera*
- (partial — *Crónica de una muerte anunciada*)

## In tension with
