# Stefan Zweig

## One-line frame
Psychological compression. Anti-Tolstoy: the great individual moment against history's smoke. Dostoevsky's depth without the religious crisis. The biographies (Fouché, Maria Antonieta, Napoleon) as history-as-literary-narrative.
