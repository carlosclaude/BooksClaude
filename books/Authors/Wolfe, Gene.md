# Gene Wolfe

## One-line frame
Not in the viz. *Book of the New Sun* — four-novel series set in a far-future Earth decayed into quasi-medieval society. Unreliable narrator, dense prose, layers of meaning that reveal themselves on rereading. Wolfe is to sci-fi what Borges is to literary fiction.
