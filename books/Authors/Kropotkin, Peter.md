# Peter Kropotkin

## One-line frame
*Mutual Aid*: cooperation, not competition, drives evolution. The anti-Darwinist, anti-Nietzschean position grounded in natural-science argument rather than moral conviction.

## Parallels
- [[Bakunin, Mikhail]] — anarchist tradition; different mechanism.
