# Isaac Asimov

## One-line frame
Psychohistory = Tolstoy's history as math. Three Laws = constitutional AI. The philosophical engineer of sci-fi — builds ethical architecture and stress-tests it. Characters have personality even when subordinated to ideas; Hari Seldon isn't interchangeable.
