# Sedimentation model

## Definition
The claim that emergent structural conditions can produce outcomes that look fully designed without any architect having designed them. Each decision layer is locally rational. Each layer creates the conditions that make the next decision seem natural. The layers compress over time into the ground that the next generation treats as given. The aggregate coherence is real; the intentionality behind it is not.

## Origin
Mine. Developed as the inoculating mechanism inside the [[AI Digital Terraforming]] thesis, to preempt the conspiratorial reading and to give the essay a structural account of how no architect can still produce a coherent result.

The model is descriptively closer to Marx than to conspiracy theory. Marx does not need a cabal to explain capitalism; he needs structural conditions that produce predictable outcomes regardless of anyone's intentions. Sedimentation names the same kind of mechanism at platform-scale: no planning required, terrifyingly coherent result.

## Mechanism
Three properties:

1. **Local rationality.** Each decision makes sense within its own frame. Facebook wanted engagement metrics. Tinder wanted swipe volume. Zoom wanted market share during the pandemic. Each company optimized for a narrow goal; none of them were planning for anything larger.
2. **Conditioning forward.** Each layer creates the conditions under which the next layer's decisions seem natural. Once the self is a profile, dating apps as marketplace seem normal. Once intimacy is swipeable, Zoom thumbnails seem normal. Once bodies are thumbnails, language models seem native.
3. **Invisibility in aggregate.** No one sees the accumulation until it is already the ground you stand on. The individual layers are defensible. The aggregate is the problem. Asking "who did this?" produces a correct but useless answer: nobody did it, and yet it was done.

The geological metaphor is precise. Sediment deposits through ordinary activity (erosion, deposition, compression). It does not require an architect to produce strata. Give it enough time and the layers compact into bedrock that the next species treats as always having been there.

## Why it matters
The sedimentation model occupies a specific intellectual space that most technology critiques do not. Most critiques split into two camps:

- **Conspiracy** ("someone planned this for their own benefit") — comfortable because it has a villain, a plot, someone to confront.
- **Dismissal** ("technology is neutral, just use it responsibly") — comfortable because it requires no structural response.

Sedimentation refuses both. It insists on the aggregate coherence (against dismissal) *and* on the absence of an architect (against conspiracy). That refusal is harder to live inside than either alternative, which is exactly why it is the honest frame.

It also resists a specific failure mode of critique: *if nobody planned it, nobody is responsible, and therefore there is nothing to resist*. Pynchon sees this in *The Crying of Lot 49* — Oedipa is trapped either way, whether the Tristero is real or hallucinated. The right response to sedimentation is not passivity but permanent vigilance without the relief of a verdict.

## Related concepts
### Genealogical
- Marx's analysis of capitalism as structural condition, not cabal — the ancestor
- Bourdieu's *habitus* — the disposition produced by sedimented social position; Airoldi extends this to algorithms (see *Machine Habitus*)
- Foucault's *episteme* — the unthought conditions of thought at a given historical moment; structurally parallel to the geological frame

### Parallel
- Emergence theory in complexity science — mathematical backing for how local rules can produce global coherence
- Evolutionary biology's *Carboniferous pattern* — plants produce oxygen as byproduct of their own activity, insects grow enormous in the new atmospheric conditions, the atmosphere eventually stabilizes and the giant insects go extinct. Nobody designed the sequence. The pattern is the essay's opening metaphor.

### In tension with
- Great-man history and its descendants (tech-founder mythology) — sedimentation specifically denies that any individual's decisions explain the aggregate
- Simple conspiracy theories — sedimentation explains the appearance of design without requiring designers

## Counter-concepts
- Intentional design (architect model) — a single mind or coordinated group planning the result
- Random accident — the outcomes would be different if the same layers had been deposited in a different order; sedimentation produces *specific* coherence, not noise

## Open questions
- Whether sedimentation is a genuinely distinct mechanism or a rebadging of older structural accounts (Marx, Foucault, Bourdieu). My claim: the platform-scale version is specific enough to deserve its own name, because the speed and visibility of the layering is historically new.
- Whether naming the model itself disrupts it. Fisher's worry about *Capitalist Realism* applies here — critiques get absorbed. The sedimentation diagnosis may already be priced in to the very platforms it describes.
- Whether the model generalizes beyond the digital environment. My guess is yes — it should apply to any situation where local rationality compounds into structural condition. Candidate domains: climate change, housing markets, educational credentialism.
